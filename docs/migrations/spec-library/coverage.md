## Acceptance-boundary

U7 integrates the complete source bodies, full historical comments, offered investment contract and approved amendments into the source-block mappings below. Worker selections are normalized into exact hashed source envelopes with explicit clause/legacy splits; their source bytes are not rewritten or normalized. The cross-owner table resolves sender handoffs to actual receiver requirements, acceptance examples and bounded tasks. Review is source-to-meaning comparison, not an inference from issue/ID counts. An issue replacement index, all-REQ task coverage or a passing link/DAG check alone would not establish this result.

Local checks and their limits are recorded in [verification](verification.md). U8's authority switch and the 12 reserved policy anchors remain pending; no unresolved anchor is reported as existing. Nothing here authorizes retirement before verified merge or claims product implementation.

The immutable [source baseline](source-baseline.json) contains exactly #1–43/#45–51, 50 bodies and 44 full comments. #44 is a PR. Whole-record hashes authenticate the archive, not the semantics of each rewrite. No source checkbox, issue state or source hash establishes feature completion. All task identities below are canonical; no provisional worker label is a live dependency.

## Selection-protocol

`B<n>.L<a>:<b>` selects inclusive lines of the exact decoded `capture.issues[number=n].body`, joined with original LF, excluding the final line terminator; heading is included. No trimming/Unicode normalization. Original views add four wrapper lines and are **not** this numbering. These U7 section envelopes include independent subclauses: the mapping cell explicitly splits them; they are not blanket issue-to-file rows. Occurrence is determined by the line boundaries when headings repeat. The original heading/list/paragraph and legacy labels remain visible in the archive. Hashes below are SHA-256 of these exact selected UTF-8 envelopes. Structural headings/blanks/navigation are non-normative unless the mapping explicitly names their substantive scope. Historical comments use captured identity and entire decoded body including trailing newlines; inner original-description checksum is not the full-comment hash.

Disposition vocabulary: **P** preserved, **C** consolidated at the named sole owner, **S** split among named destinations, **X** superseded with reason, **N** non-normative history/navigation. Review below is an integration comparison of the listed obligation, not a product test. Existing policy anchors resolve; finer anchors are reserved, not claimed present. Source-derived policy requirements remain binding regardless of pending anchor work.

## Normalized-worker-blocks

The worker source keys are integrated below as **source envelopes**, not re-extracted competing issue records. Each envelope retains the exact source lines, heading context and all embedded legacy labels. Compound cells enumerate the clause split and sole owners. A worker's smaller exact-line/range selection resolves inside its envelope by the same issue/comment identity and original ordinal; its bytes are unchanged, not replaced by the identifying quote or the rewritten destination. Envelope hashes are additional reproducible integrity evidence, not a claim that their digest equals a worker's selected-line digest. Source tuples can be expanded from the archive without the temporary packets: the selected original heading supplies the exact short quote and full heading path; its line boundary disambiguates occurrence, paragraph/list ordinal and repeated legacy labels. For each contained clause, its source label and ordinal plus the parent envelope and explicitly split destination preserve the mapping; compute its smaller digest over that exact selected substring, never over rewritten prose.

Canonical shorthand in destination cells expands inclusively within the named prefix: `REC 001/003` or `REC001/003` means REC-REQ-001 and REC-REQ-003; `AC001/003` after a prefix means that prefix's AC. Fully qualified IDs take precedence and never change owner. Original source titles/legacy IDs remain historical, not live requirements. Technical collection/library/transport suggestions are role=proposal with non-normative disposition unless the row retains their approved observable outcome.
